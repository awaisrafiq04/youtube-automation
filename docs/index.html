YouTube Automation

YouTube Automation is a private utility used by its owner to upload prepared videos to the owner's YouTube channel on a schedule.

What the application does

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to upload videos and their titles, descriptions, tags, category, and privacy setting to the YouTube channel selected by the owner. It does not provide public user accounts and is not offered as a third-party service.

Account control

Access is granted only after the channel owner signs in through Google's authorization screen. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account security settings.
