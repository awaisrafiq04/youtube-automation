YouTube Automation

Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Purpose and scope

YouTube Automation is a private application used solely by its owner to upload prepared videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It is not a public service and does not accept registrations from other users.

Information accessed

The application requests the Google OAuth permission needed to upload videos to YouTube. It processes the selected video file and associated publishing metadata, including title, description, tags, category, and privacy status. It receives the YouTube video identifier returned after a successful upload.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the owner, upload the video requested by the owner, and record whether that upload succeeded. The application does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored as private environment secrets in systems controlled by the owner. Video files and publishing metadata are stored in the owner's private storage and database. Credentials are not placed in public source code. Data is transmitted to Google and YouTube only as required to perform an authorized upload.

Retention and deletion

Upload metadata is retained for operational recordkeeping until the owner deletes it. The owner may delete stored videos and metadata at any time. OAuth access can be revoked from the Google Account's third-party connections page; stored credentials can then be deleted from the owner's local environment and automation secrets.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the user-support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
