YouTube Automation

Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Purpose and scope

YouTube Automation is a private application used solely by its owner to manage and upload prepared videos to YouTube channels authorized by the owner. It is not a public service and does not offer public signup.

Information accessed

The application requests Google OAuth permissions to identify an authorized YouTube channel and upload videos to it. It processes the selected video file, channel name and identifier, and publishing metadata including title, description, tags, category, and privacy status. It receives the YouTube video identifier after an upload.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the owner, upload the video requested by the owner, and record whether that upload succeeded. The application does not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties.

Storage and security

Dashboard authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted before database storage, and the decryption key is kept in private server and automation secrets. Video files and metadata are stored in private storage and database records protected by access controls. Credentials are not placed in public source code. Data is transmitted to Google and YouTube only for authorized channel access and uploads.

Retention and deletion

Upload metadata is retained for operational recordkeeping until the owner deletes it. The owner may delete stored videos and metadata at any time. OAuth access can be revoked from the Google Account's third-party connections page; stored credentials can then be deleted from the owner's local environment and automation secrets.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the user-support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
